# reelbot privacy policy

reelbot is a personal automation tool run by Conor Petersen for his own Instagram account (@conor_sends).
It is not offered to anyone else.

- **Google Drive:** reelbot reads and writes files only inside the `reelbot` folder its owner shares with it
  (video clips, rendered reels, and the mascot and trend files). It does not read anything else in the Drive account.
- **Instagram:** reelbot publishes trial reels to its owner's account and reads that account's own reel statistics.
- **AI services:** frames from clips and reel text are sent to Anthropic (Claude) and fal.ai to tag clips, write
  text and generate animation.
- **Storage:** stats and posting history are kept in this repository (`data/ledger.json`). No data is sold or shared
  with anyone else.
